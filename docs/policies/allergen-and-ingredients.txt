Table & Thyme Allergen and Ingredients Policy

Declared allergens
Menu descriptions list allergens that the kitchen has declared for each dish. The menu may not list every ingredient or trace source. Ask the restaurant team about an item before ordering if you have an allergy or dietary concern.

Cross-contact
The kitchen handles multiple ingredients and cannot guarantee that a dish is free from allergens. Cross-contact cannot be ruled out, including during preparation, cooking, and service. No dish should be represented as allergen-safe.

Ingredient questions
Ingredient information is limited to the current menu description and declared allergen list. If the uploaded menu information does not identify an ingredient, staff confirmation is required.
